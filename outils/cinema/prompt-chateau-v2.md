## Prompt (16:9)

```
One single continuous shot, no cuts, 30 seconds, 16:9. A smooth, steady camera journey from the sky above a château to a writing desk inside it, in warm late-afternoon sunlight. Throughout the whole shot, keep the main subject centered in the middle of the image.

0–4s: Aerial drone view at golden hour of an elegant white limestone French château with grey slate roofs and tall chimneys, in the Loire Valley. A long straight pale gravel alley leads to the entrance, lined with tall slender cypress trees and geometric boxwood parterres. The drone glides forward along the alley at a steady pace, descends and ends at eye level in front of tall glazed double doors, wide open.

4–6.5s: The camera glides through the open doors into a bright stone entrance hall with a black and white marble checkerboard floor, passes a grand curved limestone staircase with a black wrought-iron and gold railing and a black and gold lantern, then turns to the right to face a pale blue-grey boiserie wall where three slim, simple gilded picture frames hang side by side at the same height. Each frame is a thin, plain, classic gold moulding with no carvings and no ornaments, lit by a small brass picture light above it, and holds an empty plain ivory canvas: no painting, no picture, completely empty.

6.5–8s: The camera stops and holds perfectly still, straight-on to the three frames, all three fully visible and centered.

8–10.5s: The camera glides to the right into a sunlit salon with ivory boiserie, a crystal chandelier, sage green velvet armchairs and tall open French windows onto a flowering garden. It approaches a marble-topped coffee table and rises smoothly to look straight down at a large closed book lying flat in the center of the table. The book is bound in deep burgundy leather with gilded page edges. Its cover is embossed in gold foil, centered: at the top a small minimalist logo of a mountain range drawn as a single thin line with three peaks, below it the word "Qualimatic" in an elegant script, and below that the words "Tarifs 2027-2028" in small refined capitals, all inside a thin ornamental gold border.

10.5–12s: Top-down view, the camera holds perfectly still above the closed book, the cover fully visible, centered and aligned with the edges of the image.

12–13.5s: Locked-off top-down camera, absolutely no camera movement from here until 21.5s. The front cover opens slowly by itself, turning from right to left, and the book comes to rest open and perfectly flat on two empty ivory pages framed by a thin gold border.

13.5–15s: The open book stays perfectly still.

15–16s: One page turns slowly by itself from right to left and settles flat.

16–17.5s: The open book stays perfectly still.

17.5–18.5s: A second page turns slowly by itself from right to left and settles flat.

18.5–20s: The open book stays perfectly still.

20–21.5s: The book closes gently by itself, the cover turning back from left to right, and rests closed with the gold cover facing up.

21.5–27s: The camera rises slowly from the closed book and glides to an antique Louis XV writing desk near the sunlit window, with a sage green leather desk pad, then comes to rest looking straight down at a large sheet of thick ivory writing paper lying flat in the center: completely empty, no writing, no lines. Around the sheet: a cut-crystal inkwell with dark ink at the top left, a small brass seal stamp on the right, and a small vase of white flowers at the top edge of the desk.

27–30s: The camera holds perfectly still, top-down, the empty sheet of paper centered and aligned with the edges of the image.

Smooth camera movements at a constant speed, no shaking, no sudden turns, no cuts. The scene is completely empty of people and hands. The picture frames, the book pages and the sheet of paper stay completely empty the whole time; the only text in the whole video is "Qualimatic" and "Tarifs 2027-2028" on the book cover. Steady lighting with no flicker.

Style: an elegant 18th-century French château, bright and airy, warm late-afternoon sunlight streaming through tall windows. Palette of ivory boiserie, soft sage green, powder blue-grey, aged gold leaf, crystal, black and white marble, with one deep burgundy accent (the book). Refined, timeless, editorial interior photography. Photorealistic, 35mm lens, cinematic, symmetrical and calm composition. Clean, sharp image without film grain, noise or lens flare.
```
